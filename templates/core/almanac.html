The Almanac

What collectors know about this material, written down and signed.

An entry is a piece about one county, one era, one licence type or one particular licence — what was issued, how to tell an original from a reproduction, and what a run of them is actually worth knowing. It publishes under the writer’s name with their holding beside it, because that is a reader’s only way to weigh what they say. Corrections go to the author first.

Not built yet

The Almanac needs a model that does not exist — an entry, an author, a subject and a corrections queue. It sits third in the build order, after price history and collection sets, because both of those are load-bearing for screens across the rest of the site and this one is not.

It is in the navigation because the shape of the site is settled and a zone that quietly appears later is harder to plan around than one that says what it will be.

In the meantime

Two things already carry some of this. Reference pages hold what is known about a state and its issuing units, and every listing carries its seller’s own description of the object.
